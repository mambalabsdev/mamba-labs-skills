# LinkedIn Authority

**Post, comment and connect in a way that makes the outbound land.**

## Who it is for

Founders and operators whose social presence and whose outbound are currently two unrelated activities.

## The problem

Social content is written for reach and outbound is sent to a list, and the two never meet. The profile that a cold prospect checks is a resume, the posts have no specifics, and the connection campaign double touches people the email sequence is already working.

## What is in it

This pack treats the profile as the landing page for outbound, and the posts and comments as the reason a name is familiar. The connection campaign shares its exit gate with the email sequence, so one reply stops both.

Each skill in this pack reads the files its Shared context section names, from
`BRAND.md`, `ICP.md` and `VOICE.md`, where they exist. `brand-context-builder` writes
