# The SEO And Content Engine

**Pages and posts that exist because there is something on them worth reading.**

## Who it is for

Teams publishing at scale who need each page to justify itself, and anyone whose content program stalled.

## The problem

Programmatic pages go thin when the template has nothing per page but the variable, comparison pages get ignored when the competitor wins nothing, and original research goes unpublished because it feels too small.

## What is in it

The pack designs from the data source backward. Page sets are planned around what genuinely differs per entity and how it refreshes; comparison pages are sourced and dated; original research is published with a method section that earns citations. The agent readability audit covers the readers who never render the page.

## Shared context

Every skill in this pack reads `BRAND.md`, `ICP.md` and `VOICE.md` from your
project root where they exist. `brand-context-builder` writes all three from one
interview, and output quality compounds across the pack as those files improve.
